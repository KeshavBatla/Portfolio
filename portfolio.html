About me

I'm a third-year CS student at Thapar University, building agentic AI systems that actually run — not toy demos. My focus is backend and integration work: LangGraph pipelines, LLM orchestration, vector databases, and third-party API wiring.

My biggest project right now is a multi-agent cold outreach system that's gone from sketch to working prototype — it's sent real emails to real companies. Before that I shipped a RAG Q&A assistant on Streamlit Cloud, debugging session isolation and dependency conflicts in production.

Outside code: I've co-founded two e-commerce ventures — a home décor lighting store and a customizable luggage covers brand — handling sourcing, storefronts, and marketing end-to-end. I also serve as Vice-Proctor of my university hostel, managing resident welfare and student–administration relations.
